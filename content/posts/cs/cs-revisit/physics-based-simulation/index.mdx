## Shape Representation and Time Integration

主要表达方式有 Algebraic Equation, Signed Distance Field (SDF), Mesh, Particles，这里简单讲一下 SDF。

SDF 指的是用一个标量函数

$$
\operatorname{SDF}(p)=
\begin{cases}
-d(p,\partial\Omega), & p\text{ 在物体内部}\\
0, & p\text{ 在物体表面}\\
+d(p,\partial\Omega), & p\text{ 在物体外部}
\end{cases}
$$

来描述空间中任意一个点到物体的距离，$d$ 是距离函数，$\partial\Omega$ 是物体的边界。

关于物体的 $x$ 和 $v$ 向量，我们可以直接对每个点做展开，摊平成一个大向量：

<StateVectorsFigure />

然后对应的，我们把 $M$ 摊成一个大矩阵，每个 $x_i, v_i$ 对应的那个质量就是 $M_{ii}$，对于二维来说就是

$$
M = \begin{bmatrix}
m_1 & & & & \\
& m_1 & & & \\
& & m_2 & & \\
& & & m_2 & \\
& & & & \ddots
\end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{2n \times 2n}
$$

那么我们有牛顿第二定律

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dot{x} &= v\\
M\dot{v} &= f
\end{aligned}
$$

那么一种简单方法解这个方程，显然是

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{n+1} &= x^{n} + \Delta t v^{n}\\
v^{n+1} &= v^{n} + \Delta t M^{-1}f^{n}
\end{aligned}
$$

这个就是 **Forward Euler**。

这里有一个问题，可能需要用到物理知识，我来尝试解释一下。就是我们从 Hamitonian 的视角看这个转移：

$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{n+1} &= q_n + \frac{\partial H}{\partial p}(q_n, p_n)\Delta t\\
p_{n+1} &= p_{n}-\frac{\partial H}{\partial q}(q_n, p_n)\Delta t
\end{aligned}
$$

我们希望

$$
\left(\frac{\partial z_{n+1}}{\partial z_n}\right)^{\top}\Omega\left(\frac{\partial z_{n+1}}{\partial z_n}\right) = \Omega
$$

$$
\frac{\partial (q_{n+1}, p_{n+1})}{\partial(q_n, p_n)}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
1 + \frac{\partial^2 H}{\partial q_n\partial p_n}\Delta t & \frac{\partial^2 H}{\partial p_n^2}\Delta t\\
-\frac{\partial^2 H}{\partial q_n^2}\Delta t & 1 - \frac{\partial^2 H}{\partial p_n\partial q_n}\Delta t
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\left(
\frac{\partial (q_{n+1}, p_{n+1})}{\partial(q_n, p_n)}
\right)^{\top}\Omega\left(
\frac{\partial (q_{n+1}, p_{n+1})}{\partial(q_n, p_n)}
\right) = 
\left[
1+
\left(
\frac{\partial^2H}{\partial q_n^2}
\frac{\partial^2H}{\partial p_n^2}
-
\left(
\frac{\partial^2H}{\partial q_n\partial p_n}
\right)^2
\right)\Delta t^2
\right]\Omega
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{n+1} &= q_n + \frac{\partial H}{\partial p_{n+1}}\Delta t\\
p_{n+1} &= p_{n}-\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_n}\Delta t
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\frac{\partial (q_{n+1}, p_{n+1})}{\partial(q_n, p_n)}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
1 + \frac{\partial^2 H}{\partial q_n\partial p_n}\Delta t & \frac{\partial^2 H}{\partial p_n^2}\Delta t\\
-\frac{\partial^2 H}{\partial q_n^2}\Delta t & 1 - \frac{\partial^2 H}{\partial p_n\partial q_n}\Delta t
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\left(
\frac{\partial (q_{n+1}, p_{n+1})}{\partial(q_n, p_n)}
\right)^{\top}\Omega\left(
\frac{\partial (q_{n+1}, p_{n+1})}{\partial(q_n, p_n)}
\right) = 
\left[
1+
\left(
\frac{\partial^2H}{\partial q_n^2}
\frac{\partial^2H}{\partial p_n^2}
-
\left(
\frac{\partial^2H}{\partial q_n\partial p_n}
\right)^2
\right)\Delta t^2
\right]\Omega
$$
